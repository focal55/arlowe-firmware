## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug, factual] Fetch size in section 2**
- The plan said "about 230 MiB". The Pi set is 178,122,788 bytes (about 170 MiB), matching INSTALL.md. The kernel's check 7 adds about 75 MiB, per verify-third-party's usage text.
- The runbook states both separately, and gives the fresh-host command as `ARLOWE_KERNEL_FETCH=1 ARLOWE_PI_ARCHIVE_FETCH=1 scripts/verify-third-party.sh`, because `build-image.sh` also needs the kernel cache pointer.

**2. [Rule 2 - Missing Critical] The resolve-only list is hard-coded**
- Section 4 step 7 says a new removal marker needs `pi_archive_record_candidate`'s `--resolve-only` list edited.
- Without that note, the check the plan asked for would find the marker, and the operator would then expect a record build to fix it. It wouldn't.

**3. [Adapted] rpi-eeprom sign-off scope**
- Sign-off is extended to `raspi-utils-eeprom` and `raspi-firmware`. Both are in the manifest, and both are bootloader-path packages.

**4. [Size] 389 lines against an estimate of 250**
- This is under the 400 cap. The plan said not to trim for size.
- The overrun is section 3: the plan's five failure groups expand to about 25 distinct messages once they are quoted from the code.
