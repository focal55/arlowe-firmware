<objective>
Reconcile the Phase 6 requirements/ROADMAP text with two research findings that contradict it, BEFORE any build work happens. These are owner-facing spec changes (BOM/sizing + boot-selector mechanism), so they are visible, reviewable deliverables — not silent build-time decisions.

Two reconciliations:
1. **16 GB -> 32 GB floor.** Measured model artifacts make two equal-sized, model-laden rootfs slots impossible on a 16 GB card (~26.5 GB for Qwen 7B, ~16.6 GB even for 1.5B; both exceed a 16 GB card's ~14.8 GB usable). IMAGE-04 ("<= 16 GB target") and PART-05 ("within 16 GB budget") are physically unsatisfiable under the locked "models baked into each slot" + "equal-sized A/B" decisions.
2. **tryboot root= swap, not U-Boot/active.txt.** PART-02/SC3 say the selector "reads a flag (U-Boot env or /boot/active.txt)". The locked single-shared-/boot tryboot design instead flips the persistent default by editing the boot `root=` in `config.txt`/`cmdline.txt`. The one-shot `tryboot.txt` path is reserved for Phase 9 OTA trial-boot.

Purpose: Downstream plans build to 32 GB and a root=-swap selector; the docs must agree before that, or the phase ships against a stale spec.
Output: Two ADRs + amended REQUIREMENTS.md (IMAGE-03/04, PART-02/05) + amended ROADMAP.md Phase 6 SC2/SC3/SC5.
</objective>

  <name>Task 2: ADR-0005 — A/B selector tryboot root= swap</name>
  <files>docs/architecture/0005-ab-selector-tryboot-root-swap.md</files>
  <action>
Write an ADR recording the boot-selector mechanism, correcting PART-02/SC3.

Context: PART-02/SC3 wording ("U-Boot env or /boot/active.txt") is wrong for the locked Pi-native tryboot, single-shared-/boot design. Summarize 06-RESEARCH.md GATE 2: Pi 5 firmware A/B has two styles — partition-level (rejected; needs two FAT boot partitions) and file-level within one boot partition (`tryboot_a_b=1`, firmware loads `tryboot.txt` instead of `config.txt` on the one-shot path). `reboot "0 tryboot"` is ONE-SHOT and reverts on power-cycle, so a persistent default flip cannot use it.

Decision: The persistent A/B default is the boot `root=` in the shared `/boot/firmware` config (`cmdline.txt` / inline `cmdline=` in `config.txt`). `arlowe-ab` flips the persistent default by rewriting `root=` to point at the target slot's PARTUUID, then rebooting. `tryboot_a_b=1` + `tryboot.txt` + `reboot "0 tryboot"` are RESERVED for the Phase 9 OTA one-shot trial-boot path — not used for the v1 persistent flip. No U-Boot, no custom initramfs, no `active.txt` flag file.

Consequences: `arlowe-ab` (plan 06-05) owns the `root=` rewrite as the seam Phase 9 reuses. The slot-B recovery stub (plan 06-05) self-heals by rewriting `root=` back to slot A. Note the Pi-5 freeze-on-corrupt-kernel caveat (power-cycle recovers) for the recovery framing.
  </action>
