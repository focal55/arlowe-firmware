Lines split on `\n`, `\r` or `\r\n`; a line whose first non-space character is `#` is a comment; the key ends at the first `:` or `=`; the value's leading whitespace and its trailing unescaped whitespace are stripped; `\` escapes (`\\`, `\b \f \n \r \t \v`, octal `\NNN`, any other `\c` → `c`, a trailing lone `\` dropped). <feature>
  <name>NetworkManager wrapper: radio, scan, AP, profiles</name>
  <files>runtime/pair/netman.py, runtime/pair/tests/test_netman.py</files>
  <behavior>
    - `session_credentials(device_id)` → `(ssid, psk)`; ssid is `Arlowe-Setup-` + first 4 chars; psk is 12 chars from the alphabet; two calls give different psks.
    - `radio_on()` runs `nmcli radio wifi on`.
    - `scan()` runs `nmcli -t -f SSID,SIGNAL,SECURITY device wifi list --rescan yes`, parses escaped colons, drops empty SSIDs, dedupes by strongest signal, returns `[{"ssid", "signal", "secure"}]` sorted by signal.
    - `ap_up(ssid, psk)` runs the `add save no` call (no psk in it, `connection.uuid <uuid4>`), then `connection up uuid <uuid> passwd-file /dev/stdin` with the psk on stdin. `ap_down()` runs `nmcli connection down uuid <uuid>` then `delete uuid <uuid>`, tolerating "not found".
    - `wifi_profiles()` → UUIDs of every `802-11-wireless` connection from `nmcli -t -f UUID,TYPE connection show`. `delete_profile(uuid)`.
    - Logging records the action and outcome only.
  </behavior>
  <implementation>`NetMan(runner=_default_runner, nmcli="nmcli")`. The runner receives argv lists and optional stdin bytes; tests point `nmcli` at the fake.</implementation>
</feature>
