<execution_notes>
- Research Pattern 1's `arlowe-pair.service` bullet list is the spec, with these additions: `Wants=arlowe-radio-init.service`, `After=arlowe-radio-init.service arlowe-factory-reset-resume.service`, `Environment=ARLOWE_BROKER_FILE=/boot/firmware/arlowe-broker.json`, `Environment=ARLOWE_WHISPLAY_DRIVER_PATH=/opt/arlowe/third_party/whisplay-driver`, `Environment=PYTHONPATH=/opt/arlowe/runtime:/opt/arlowe/runtime/lib`, `Environment=PYTHONUNBUFFERED=1`, `Restart=on-failure`, `RestartSec=5`.
- `ExecStart=/usr/bin/python3 -m pair` uses the declared python3 floor; no allowlist entry.
- `ReadWritePaths=/etc/arlowe /var/lib/arlowe/identity /var/lib/arlowe/dashboard /var/lib/arlowe/state /var/lib/arlowe/logs`. `/run/arlowe-pair` comes from `RuntimeDirectory=arlowe-pair` (mode 0750); `WorkingDirectory=/run/arlowe-pair` (lgpio writes scratch files relative to the CWD).
- No `Requires=` on identity-init: a failed identity must show on the Whisplay, not silently prevent pairing (08-23 shows it).
- `DeviceAllow`: copy face's block verbatim, including `char-gpiochip` and its comment (memory: DeviceAllow with a node name or glob grants nothing, silently).
- **No `Conflicts=` (B1).** Both units are `WantedBy=multi-user.target`, and `Condition*` is evaluated when a job runs, after the boot transaction has resolved a conflict in favour of the declaring unit, so `Conflicts=arlowe-face.service` would drop the face's start job on every boot of a paired unit. The display handoff is the daemon's: paired screen, hold, `board.cleanup()`, `systemctl start --no-block` the six, exit 0 (08-20 `start_runtime`, 08-23). Say this in a comment in the unit where a reader would look for `Conflicts=`.
- `SupplementaryGroups=gpio spi video` and the face's three `SystemCallFilter=` lines verbatim (`@system-service`, `~@privileged @resources`, `mbind`) with its comment: on the Pi 5 the fake-NUMA kernel calls `mbind`, which `@system-service` does not allow, and the process dies with SIGSYS (memory: Pi 5 substrate traps).
</execution_notes>
